Bread takes 4 essential ingredients:

- **[[Flour|Flour]]**: Base of the bread. Use any amount. 250g is a small bread.
- **[[Salt|Salt]]**: 2% of the flour's weight.
- **[[Water|Water]]**: Use warm water for a faster rise, cold for slower (biology).
	- 60% of flour: dense, chewy, like bagels.
	- 70% of flour: medium bubbliness
	- 80% of flour: very bubbly, like focaccia.
- **[[Yeast|Yeast]]**: 0.2% to 1% of flour. More means a faster rise.

Let it rise until it's double in size, then 220C for about 30 mins. (hard to overcook)
Let it sit for 30 mins.
