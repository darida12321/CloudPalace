
Based on depth, the sea can be subdivided into several regions:
- [[Sunlight zone]] (Epipelagic, 0m-200m): Enough sunlight for [[Photosynthesis|photosynthesis]] to happen. 
- [[Twilight zone]] (Mesopelagic, 200m-1km): Still some light, but no [[Photosynthesis|photosynthesis]].
- [[Midnight zone]] ( Bathypelagic, 1km-4km): No light whatsoever. Cold. High pressure.
- [[Abyssal zone]] (Abyssopelagic, 4km-6km): Close to the [[Abyssal plain|abyssal plain]].
- [[Hadal zone]] (Hadopelagic, 6km-): Oceanic trenches, which condense organic materials.

The bottom of the ocean is mostly an empty plain, but has some interesting features: 
- [[Abyssal plain]]: Organic sediment settles over years, ready to be consumed.
- [[Hydrothermal vent|Hydrothermal vents]]: Where oceanic plates diverge, sulphites get released.
- [[Cold seep|Cold seeps]]:  Where oceanic plates converge, methane gets released.
- [[Whale-fall|Whale-falls]]: Along whale migration patterns, bone-decomposing bacteria spread.
- [[Wood-fall|Wood-falls]]: Fallen ships also give rise to unique ecosystems.

##### Life
There are plenty of life under the surface. Most of these are quite unique and interesting in their own right. Some examples are [[Barnacle|Barnacles]], [[Jellyfish|Jellyfish]], [[Sponge|Sponges]], [[Coral|Coral]], [[Siphonophore|Siphonophores]] or [[Sea squirt|Sea squirts]].
