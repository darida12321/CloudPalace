##### Ingredients
- 1kg boneless beef short ribs, cut to 1.5cm chunks
- [[Salt|salt]] and [[Pepper|pepper]]
- 2tbsp canola oil
- 2 carrots peeled, split, sliced to 1.5cm
- 2stalk celery split, sliced to 1.5cm
- 1 onion finely diced
- 1/2tsp Marmite
- 1tsp soy sauce
- 2 clove garlic
- 1tbsp tomato paste
- 1l (4cup) [[Chicken stock|chicken stock]]  (homemade or low-sodium canned)
- 400g can whole tomatoes, chopped
- 1cup pearl barley
- 2 bay leaves
- 4cup torn kale leaves
##### Steps
1. Toss short ribs with salt and pepper. 
2. Heat oil in Dutch oven high heat, until smoking. Cook beef without moving. (5min)
3. Stir the beef, continue cooking (10min). Put aside
4. Medium-high heat add carrot, celery, onion. Cook while stirring. (4 mins)
5. Add Marmite, soy sauce, garlic, tomato paste (1 min)
6. Add stock, scrape browned bits with wooden spoon.
7. Add tomato, barley, bay leaves, beef. High heat to boil.
8. Lowest possible heat, cover the pot with a slight gap. Cook 2h, occasionally stirring.
9. Add kale, cook while stirring (2min)
