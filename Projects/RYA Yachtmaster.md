It's the ultimate goal for skippers. A proof of experience and skill. 

**Yachtmaster Coastal**: For coastal cruises, but not longer passages. ([requirements](https://www.rya.org.uk/training/certificates-of-competence/yachtmaster-coastal-exam/))
**Yachtmaster Offshore**: For passages less than 150miles from shore. ([requirements](https://www.rya.org.uk/training/certificates-of-competence/yachtmaster-offshore-exam/))
**Yachtmaster Ocean**: For vessels up to 200gt and passages of any length. ([requirements](https://www.rya.org.uk/training/certificates-of-competence/yachtmaster-ocean-exam/))
