##### Ingredients
- 3tbsp unflavoured [[Gelatin|gelatin]]
- 1kg [[Chicken|chicken]] back and wing tips, preferably from young chicken.
- 1kg [[Chicken|chicken]] legs.
- 1 large onion, roughly chopped
- 1 large carrot, roughly chopped
- 2 stalk celery, roughly chopped
- 2 bay leaves
- 2tsp whole black peppercorn
- 1tsp fennel seed
- 1tsp coriander seed
- 6 sprig parsley.
##### Steps
1. 4 cup water and gelatin. Set aside for 10 mins.
2. Chop chicken back and wing tips with cleaver, then food processor until ground chicken.
3. Add everything to a dutch oven, and cold water to cover the ingredients.
4. Simmer for 45 mins. Add water if necessary. Ladle scum off.
5. Transfer chicken legs to bowl. Fine-mash strain the stock. Reduce to about 2l (10 mins?)
6. Take meat off chicken legs, discard bones. Use meat for something.
7. Rest for 15 mins, skim fat off with ladle. Or refrigerate overnight until fat solidifies.
##### Notes
[[Chicken]] **carcasses** is a cheap, and really good alternative
Will keep for 5 days, or 3 months frozen.
