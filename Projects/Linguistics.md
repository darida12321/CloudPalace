The core layers of the language are:
- [[Phonetics]]: The study of sounds.
- [[Phonology]]: The study of how sounds combine.
- [[Morphology]]: The study of the smallest elements of meaning.
- [[Syntax]]: The study of how [[Morpheme|morphemes]] combine into larger structures.
- [[Semantics]]: The study of context-free meaning. 
- [[Pragmatics]]: The study of meaning in context.
