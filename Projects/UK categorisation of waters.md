The UK divides waters into 7 categories. If you ride a British flagged boat, you must adhere to the required certifications.

| -------- | --- | --- |
| Category 0 | Unrestricted | [[RYA Yachtmaster\|RYA Yachmaster Ocean]] |
| Category 1 | 150 miles from safe haven | [[RYA Yachtmaster\|RYA Yachmaster Offshore]] |
| Category 2 | 60 miles from safe haven | [[RYA Yachtmaster\|RYA Yachmaster Offshore]] |

| Category 3 | 20 miles from safe haven | [[RYA Yachtmaster\|RYA Yachmaster]] |
| ^^ | ^^ | RYA Advanced Powerboat |

| Category 4 | 20 miles from safe haven,<br>in favourable weather and daylight | [[RYA Yachtmaster\|RYA Yachmaster]] |
| ^^ | ^^ | RYA Advanced Powerboat |

| ^^ | ^^ | RYA Advanced Powerboat |
