A 5-day [course](https://www.rya.org.uk/course-finder/day-skipper-practical-sailing/) resulting with you being able to drive a 30-45ft sail cruising yacht on day trips. You'll learn how to drive a yacht.

To attend, you need to have spent 5 days and 100 miles on a sailing yacht, and the [[RYA Day Skipper Theory course|RYA Day Skipper Theory course]] first. Afterwards, you'll be eligible to apply for an [[ICC (International Certificate for Operators of Pleasure Craft)|ICC]]
