Most **animals** consist of:
- **Muscle**: What we typically think of as meat.
	- **Slow-twitch muscle**: These are for sustained movement. These require oxygen to function (**aerobic**), and are dense with capillaries. They are darker.
	- **Fast-twitch muscle**: Short bursts of intense movement. They don't require oxygen to function (**anaerobic**), so they have less capillaries and a paler colour.
- **Fat**: provides insulation and energy storage. It's also where most of the flavour is.
- **Connective tissue**: Made of **collagen** and other tissue strands that are woven together. Heating it up unravels them, turning it into **gelatin**. This forms a loose matrix, giving [[Stock|stock]] and [[Sauce|sauces]] its body and texture.
- **Bone**: What gives animals structure.
