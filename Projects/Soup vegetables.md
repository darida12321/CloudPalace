Different vegetables need to be **prepared** differently, and cooked for a different **time**.

| Vegetable          | Prep                                         | Cooking time |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Carrot             | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Cauliflover        | Separate to florets, slice 0.75cm thick      | 20 mins      |
| Celery             | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Celery Root        | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Jicama             | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Kohlrabi           | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Leek               | Thinly slice or dice                         | 20 mins      |
| Onion              | Thinly slice or dice                         | 20 mins      |
| Parsnip            | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Potato             | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Radish             | Cut to 1.5cm chunks                          | 20 mins      |
| Rutabaga           | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Sweet potato       | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 20 mins      |
| Asparagus          | Cut to 3cm lengths                           | 10 mins      |
| Bell pepper        | Cut to 1.5cm chunks                          | 10 mins      |
| Broccoli           | Separate florets, slice 0.75cm thick         | 10 mins      |
| Butternut Squash   | Peel and cut to 1.5cm chunks                 | 10 mins      |
| Cabbage            | Slice 0.4cm thick                            | 10 mins      |
| Collard Greens     | Roughly chop leaves, cut stems to 3cm        | 10 mins      |
| Green beans        | Trim and cut to 3cm pieces                   | 10 mins      |
| Kale               | Roughly chop leaves, cut stems to 3cm        | 10 mins      |
| Summer Squash      | Cut to 1.5cm chunks                          | 10 mins      |
| Zucchini           | Cut to 1.5cm chunks                          | 10 mins      |
| Arugula            | Remove tough stems                           | 5 mins       |
| Brussel Sprouts    | Pull off leaves                              | 5 mins       |
| Chard              | Roughly chop leaves, cut stem to 3cm lengths | 5 mins       |
| Corn kernels       | Cut off cobs.                                | 5 mins       |
| Lima beans, frozen | None                                         | 5 mins       |
| Peas, frozen       | None                                         | 5 mins       |
| Baby spinach       | None                                         | 5 mins       |
| Curly spinach      | Roughly chop                                 | 5 mins       |
| Watercress         | Roughly chop                                 | 5 mins       |
