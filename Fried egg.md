##### Ingredients
- 1 [[Egg|egg]]
- 3tbsp olive oil
- [[Salt]] and [[Pepper|pepper]]
##### Steps
1. Transfer egg to a fine-mesh strainer if you want a more perfect shape.
2. Heat oil to 150C. Tilt the skillet and spoon oil on the egg.
3. After about 1 minute, transfer egg to towel-lined plate. Season with salt and pepper. 



# Notes
You want the yolk to be liquid (below 63C), but the white firm (above 70C). If you just place it on the pan, medium heat (150C is the best, but the yolk starts to cook while the white is still liquid.

A better, but more cumbersome approach is to spoon hot oil on the top of the white so it sets faster.
