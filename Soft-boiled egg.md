##### Ingredients:
- 1 [[Egg|egg]]
- 0.5l [[Water|water]]
##### Steps
1. Boil water.
2. Gently place eggs in the water and turn the heat off.
3. Wait for the desired amount of time. 
4. Peel the eggs while they're warm, under running water.

# Notes
You want an opaque egg white (70C-85C) but a liquid yolk (below 70C). This needs a temperature difference:
- Keep the eggs cold, directly taken from the fridge.
- Put them directly into boiling water, with the lid on.
- Turn off the heat immediately, so the outermost layer doesn't overcook.
- Make sure there is plenty of water so the temperature doesn't drop. (0.5l per egg)

##### Cooking times:
- **1-3min**: Outer white just solid enough to keep its shape. Good for salads.
- **4min**: White is opaque except near the yolk. Good topping for vegetables/grains.
- **5 min**: White is opaque, yolk is raw.
- **6 min**: White is opaque and firm, yolk starts to firm up at the edges.
- **7 min**: White is cooked and hard, yolk is golden, but setting around the edges.
