A UK governing body for **boating**. It promotes boating by providing **courses** and **certificates**. My focus will be on **sailing** specifically.

While the [[RYA Yachtmaster|Yachtmaster]] is the highest degree of sailing ability, for most practical use cases, a [[RYA Day Skipper Practical Sailing course|Day Skipper Practical Sailing certificate]] is enough. This lets you sail in [[UK categorisation of waters|category 5 and 6]] waters. It's recommended to do the [[RYA Day Skipper Theory course|Day Skipper Theory course]] beforehand. That requires at least a bit of on-boat experience, obtainable by the [[RYA Competent Crew course|Competent Crew course]].

The only international certificate is the [[ICC (International Certificate for Operators of Pleasure Craft)|ICC]]. For sailing, providing a [[RYA Day Skipper Practical Sailing course|Day Skipper Practical Sailing certificate]] is enough, which will allow you to operate **sailing boats** and **power boats** up to 10m in length.

However, a certificate is often not enough. Many rental places check for your boating CV to ensure you have adequate experience. Join a sailing club to build this up in addition to the tests.

##### Courses
- **Sail cruising**:
	- [[RYA Start Yachting course]]: Absolute fundamentals.
	- [[RYA Competent Crew course]]: Experience living on a yacht.
	- [[RYA Day Skipper Practical Sailing course]]: Drive a yacht.
	- [[RYA Coastal Skipper Practical Sailing course]]: Driving offshore, at night, on the sea.
- **Navigation**:
	- [[RYA Essential Navigation and Seamanship course]]: Basic navigation
	- [[RYA Day Skipper Theory course]]: Chartwork, navigation, etc. 
	- [[RYA Yachtmaster Offshore Theory course]]: Electronic positioning, weather forecast. 
	- [[RYA Yachtmaster Ocean Theory course]]: Ocean-based navigation.
- **Specialist**:
	- [[RYA Marine Radio SRC course]]: Learn how to use a VHF radio.
