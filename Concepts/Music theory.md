[[Note]]
Most musical instruments play sounds of different frequencies. One of these is called a **note**.

They are named A, B, C, D, E, F and G, after which they repeat. If you double the frequency (or go an [[Octave|octave]] up), you end up at the same note. The difference here can be denoted using C1, C2, ...

There are [[Sharp and flat keys|sharps and flats]] that nudge a note up or down by one. 

---
[[Chord]]
A **chord** is made up of three or more [[Note|notes]]. 

Different chords:
- **Major**: 1 - 3 - 5
- **Minor**: 1 - flat 3 - 5
- **Diminished**: 1 - flat 3 - flat 5
- **Augmented**: 1 - 3 - sharp 5


To break things up, you can play a chord in a different order ([[Inversion|inversion]]), or delay some keys [[Broken chord|broken chord]]
