# Pots and pans
Almost every recipe uses pots and pans in some form or another. They are a tool for transferring energy to food, so the **material** is very important. They also act as a container for the food, as such, they come in many **shapes**. Finally, different **brands** produce different quality products.

##### Material
The **material** needs to **conduct** heat well, otherwise the pan will have hot/cold spots. It should also have good **heat capacity** in order to cook the food evenly. Different materials also have different chemical properties. Some add a metallic flavour to acidic food, some rust when exposed to water, some can easily be scraped, some can't handle extreme heat.
