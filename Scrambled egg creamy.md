##### Ingredients:
- 3 whole [[Egg|eggs]], 1 yolk
- 1/2 tsp [[Salt|salt]]
- 1 tbsp chilled butter
- 1 tbsp heavy cream or [[Crème fraiche|crème fraiche]] 
##### Steps
1. Combine eggs, yolk and salt. Mix them, then let it sit for 15 mins.
2. Drop in the butter and cook over medium-low. Stir a lot.
3. Remove from the heat, add cream, stir for 15 sec.

# Notes
Stirring the egg causes vapor bubbles to rupture, making the eggs more dense.
High heat causes the water to evaporate and expand making the eggs more fluffy.
Adding proteins stops the egg from setting, making it more tender.

You can add protein and water to the eggs:
- Water: Creates more bubbles. fluffier but less flavour.
- Milk: Small amount of protein.
- Cream: Medium amounts of protein.
- Cold butter: Large amounts of protein. Also a heat regulator.
