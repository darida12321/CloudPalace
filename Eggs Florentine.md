##### Ingredients
- 4 [[Poached egg|poached eggs]]
- [[Hollandaise sauce]]
- 2 English muffins split, roasted
- 2tsp vegetable oil
- 1 minced garlic
- 1 bunch spinach
- Cayenne pepper
- Parsley leaves

##### Steps
1. Boil salted water, cook the poached eggs.
2. Cook garlic in vegetable oil for 30s. Add spinach with 2tbsp water, cook until wilted. 
3. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Cook the muffins in the same pan.
5. Make the Hollandaise sauce.
6. Muffin, bacon, egg, hollandaise, sprinkle of cayenne, parsley leaves.
