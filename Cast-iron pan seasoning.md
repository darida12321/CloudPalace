Seasoning a **cast-iron** pan gives it a layer of non-stick surface. This is done by putting fat on it and heating it up, which creates a layer of polymers.

##### Initial layer of seasoning
1. Rub 1/2cup of kosher salt into the pan, then wash with hot soapy water.
2. Rub every surface with corn/vegetable/canola oil. 
3. Heat up pan to 230C for 30 mins, or until the surface is distinctively darker.
4. Let it cool and repeat the process a few times.

##### Maintenance
- Use the pan often, and avoid liquid-based foods at the beginning.
- Clear pan immediately after use, to avoid tough abrasives.
- Heat the pan until it starts to smoke. Rub inside with oiled paper towel. 

##### Possible problems
- **Scaling**: When you heat the pan repeatedly without oiling, so the seasoning flakes off.
	- You have to re-season it from the start.
- **Rusting**: When you leave your pan to air-dry. 
	- You can season over the rusted bit.
