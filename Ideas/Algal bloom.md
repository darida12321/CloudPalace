These happen when plankton grow out of control. In extreme cases, it's called a **HAB** or **Harmful Algal Bloom**. This is harmful to local [[Coral|coral]], birds, fish, mammals and humans.

They can be bad because they clog the gill of fish, block sunlight, they die and decompose all at once sucking the oxygen out of the water, or release harmful toxins upon death.

It's often caused by chemical fertilizers leaking into the ocean.

These can come in many different colours: brown, green, orange, but when the plankton are red, it's called a **red tide**.
