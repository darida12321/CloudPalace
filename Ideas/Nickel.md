It's an element of the [[Periodic table|periodic table]]. **Ni**, [[Atomic number|atomic number]] 28. Silvery-white [[Metal|metal]] with golden tinge. It forms in [[Star|stars]] along with [[Iron|iron]], so they often occur together.

It was classified as an element in **1751**. It was mistaken for a [[Copper|copper]] mineral in the [[Cobalt|cobalt]] mines of [[Sweden]], because both can be green. Nickel was a mischievous sprite in German mythology. "Kupfernickel" means "Nickel's copper".

##### Sources
[[Pentlandite|Pentlandite]]: $\ce{(Fe,Ni)_9S_8}$ 

##### Usage
- 68% used for [[Stainless steel|stainless steel]].
- 10% used for **nickel** and [[Copper|copper]] based alloys.
- 9% used for plating.
- 7% for [[Alloy steel|alloy steels]].
- 3% in foundries 
- 4% used for other application like rechargeable batteries.
