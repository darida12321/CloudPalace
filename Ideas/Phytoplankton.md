**Plankton** are organisms in the ocean that can't effectively swim against the currents. **Phytoplanktons** are planktons that can [[Photosynthesis|photosynthesise]]. They have chlorophyl, but that's about all the similarity they have to plans.

A related concept is the **plankton paradox** stating that these **phytoplankton** are competing for one resource, so one specie should thrive over all others. And yet, there is an extremely large variety of species. This basically boils down to the [[Competitive exclusion principle|competitive exclusion principle]] and its many well-known flaws.

In some extreme cases, they show up as [[Algal bloom|algal blooms]].
