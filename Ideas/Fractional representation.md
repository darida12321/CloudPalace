Fractional numbers fill the number line infinitely densely. However, we only have a finite number of bits to work with. As a result, we have a tradeoff between the range and the density of the numbers we can represent.

The easiest method is the [[Fixed-point representation|fixed-point representation]], which is essentially just representing a [[Signed integer representation|signed integer]], which we divide by a power of two for our interpretation.

A lot more widely used is the [[Floating-point representation|floating-point representation]], which has a dynamic range of precision. Small numbers can be represented precisely, while bigger ones start loosing precision.
