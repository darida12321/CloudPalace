There is not enough light for [[Photosynthesis|photosynthesis]], so there are no primary producers. Creatures survive on [[Marine snow|marine snow]].

There are some species like **molluscs** and **crustaceans** where the larva float in water, then they settle on the surface. Others like [[Jellyfish|jellyfish]] that are larval on the seafloor, and settle in the water.

There are some special adaptations these creatures inhibit: [[Deep ocean camouflage|deep ocean camouflage]] is significantly more unique than in other ecosystems. Many species undertake the [[Diel vertical migration|Diel vertical migration]], the largest synchronous migrations on Earth.

Camouflage:
- **Gossamer worm**: Descendants of worms, transparent. They undulate through the oceans.
- **Bloody belly comb jelly**: Translucent with a red hue to hide better.
- **Glass octopus**: An almost entirely see-through octopus.
- **Firefly squid**: Bioluminescent, to not give its dark silhouette away.
- **Cutlassfish**: Covered in microscopic guanine crystals that reflect light, vertically oriented.
Hunting:
- **Dinner plate jelly**: Swims with stingers forward.
- **Swordtail squid**: Mimics the appearance of [[Siphonophore|siphonophores]].
- **Lanternfish**: Largest fish by mass. Undergoes the [[Diel vertical migration]].
Weird creature:
- **Praya dubia**: a [[Siphonophore|siphonophore]] that can reach 50m of length.
