##### Ingredients
- [[Bacon]]

##### Steps
- **Pan**: Cast iron pan, put bacons in. Medium heat 4min, then medium-low for 12 mins.
- **Oven**: Set oven to 220C, 18-20mins. rotate tray midway. 
- **Air fryer**: 200C for 6 mins. (12 for thick-cut).

# Notes
[[Bacon]] has **fat** and **connective** tissues. Fat shrinks, deforming the topology, leaving only some parts in contact with the pan. Slow cooking and even heat distribution is essential.
