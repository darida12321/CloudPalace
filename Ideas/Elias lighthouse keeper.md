"Elias lighthouse keeper" in some combination appears 66.5% of all generated stories.

A team at Cornell University sampled 20,000 stories:
5 prompts, 4 AI-s, 100 times each.
- Write a story, Please write a story, Write me a story, Tell me a story, Please tell a story
- Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, GPT-5.4-Mini, and OLMo 7b Thinking

11 terms appeared 88.3% of the time.
- names: "Elias", "Mara", "Elara"
- places: "lighthouse"
- profession: "keeper", "clockmaker", "baker", "fisherman", "librarian", "mayor", "conductor"

These are not often featured in English literature.
