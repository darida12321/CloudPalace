The human body is capable of producing a large variety of sounds. But this has to be carved into distinct chunks, or **phonemes**.

For example the **p** in "**pin**" and "**spin**" are slightly different (pin has a small puff of air), these aren't distinguished by the English language. These two **p**-s are **allophones** of a **phoneme**.

A basic notion is that if two sounds are interchangeable in a language, then they are the same **phoneme**. This is language-dependent, giving rise to accents.
