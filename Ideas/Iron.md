It's an element of the [[Periodic table|periodic table]]. **Fe**, [[Atomic number|atomic number]] 26. Silvery-grey [[Metal|metal]]. Reacts with water and oxygen to make hydrated iron oxides, aka. **rust**. This expands in volume and flakes off, creating new surfaces to rust.

**Iron** is the most abundant element by mass because it is one of the most stable [[Nucleus|nuclei]]. While [[Star|stars]] create other elements via [[Fusion|fusion]], they eventually decay into **iron**. It forms along with [[Nickel|nickel]], so they often occur together

##### Sources
[[Magnetite]] $\ce{Fe3O4}$ 72.4% Fe
[[Hematite]]: $\ce{Fe2O3}$ 69.9% Fe
[[Goethite]]: $\ce{FeO(OH)}$ 62.9% Fe
[[Limonite]]: $\ce{FeO(OH) n(H2O)}$ 55% Fe
[[Siderite]]: $\ce{FeCO3}$ 48.2% FE

While it makes up 5% of the [[Earth's crust|Earth's crust]], it's bound in silicate which takes a lot of energy to melt, so we get it from Hematite usually.


##### Usage
Based on the carbon content, it can be made into:
- [[Wrought iron]]: Carbon <0.08%
- [[Steel]]: Carbon 0.08%-2%
- [[Cast iron]]: Carbon >2%
