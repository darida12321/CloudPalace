Hot water has more dissolved minerals, which can give an off-flavour. Use cold water.

##### Boiling water
1. **Quivering** (55C-75C): Tiny bubbles  form along nucleation sites, quivering the surface.
2. **Subsimmer** (75C-90C): A few of the bubbles start rising, giving rise to small streams.
3. **Simmer** (90C-100C): The bubbles break the surface of the water regularly.
4. **Full boil** (100C): Bubbles escape rapidly.

At a high enough altitude, the boiling water won't cook pasta/beans/vegetables well. A pressure cooker is essential here.
