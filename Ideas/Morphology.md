The study of how words are built from [[Morpheme|morphemes]], the smallest unit of meaning. "Cats" consists of 4 [[Phoneme|phonemes]] (k, æ, t, s), but only 2 [[Morpheme|morphemes]] (cat, s).

Languages can be categorized based on which kinds of [[Morpheme|morphemes]] they use. However, these aren't clear categories as all are present in all languages.
- **Isolating**: They use one [[Morpheme|morpheme]] and vary the meaning with word order.
- **Agglutinative**: They chain [[Morpheme|bound morphemes]] to vary the meaning.
- **Fusional**: Cram several grammatical categories into a single ending.
- **Polysynthetic**: Build whole sentences as one word.
