Fatty liquid from mammals.

It is often categorized by its fat content.
- **Whole** milk: 3.25% - 3.6% fat.
- **Semi-skimmed** or **low-fat** milk: 1.5% - 2% fat. 
- **Skimmed** milk: Under 0.5% fat.

The most common one is from cows, but it can com from other animals too:
- **Sheep**: It's higher in fat, protein and nutrients.
- **Plant**: It's made by grinding and soaking grains, nuts and seeds.

Most milk undergoes **pasteurization** to increase its shelf life.
- **Raw milk**: Has the most proteins, but prone to bacterial infections.
- **Regular pasteurized**: 72C for 20 seconds. Standard for supermarkets. Shelf life of weeks.
- **Ultra High Temperature pasteurized**: 135C for 1 second. Shelf life of months.
	- Labelled **UHT** or **Ultra-Pasteurized**.
- **Low-Temperature pasteurized**: 63C for 30mins. Doesn't produce the "cooked" flavour.
