##### Ingredients
- 5 [[Egg|eggs]]
- 3/4 tsp [[salt]]. 1/4 tsp [[Pepper|pepper]]
- 2tbsp [[Butter|butter]]
- 100g diced ham steak
- 50g cheddar
##### Steps
1. Combine egg, salt, pepper. Let it sit for 15 mins.
2. Heat 1tbsp butter over medium, cook until lightly browned.
3. Cook the ham, set aside. Add cheese to it and mix it.
4. Heat 1tbsp butter over medium, cook until lightly browned. 
5. Use spatula to push sides to the centre, and let the egg flow around. 1min.
6. Take off the heat, add toppings, cover the pan until it cooks.
7. Fold omelette in half and serve.

# Notes
Other fillings also easy. 
- **Mushroom, pepper, onion**: 1/2 cup each. Cook mushrooms, then add onion and pepper.
- **Asparagus, shallot, goat cheese**: 8 stalk asparagus, cut to 5cm bits. 5min. add shallot.
