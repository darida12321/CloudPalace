##### Ingredients
- 1 [[Chicken stock]] recipe.
- 2 carrots, medium dice
- 1 stalk celery, medium dice
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2cup long-grain white rice
- 1/4cup chopped parsley
- 2cup kale torn into pieces (6 leaves)
- [[Salt|salt]] and [[Pepper|pepper]]
##### Steps
1. Combine chicken stock, carrot, celery, onion and rice in Dutch oven, bring to boil.
2. Simmer for 15 mins, add greens.
3. Cook until the rice is done and vegetables are tender (5 mins)
4. Stir in chicken meat. Add salt and pepper.
