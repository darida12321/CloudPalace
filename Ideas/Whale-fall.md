When a whale dies and fall onto the [[Abyssal plain|abyssal plain]], it spawns a new temporary ecosystem. This has several stages. First, mobile scavengers consume all flesh in a matter of months. Then other animals occupy the bones, making use of leftover organic material.

Finally, a different process happens. Boneworms cover and burrow into the skeleton. Anaerobic bacteria consume the lipids, and produce sulphide as a by-product. [[Chemosynthesis|Chemosynthetic]] bacteria consume this, and mats of them form around the area.

The lifeforms here are different from those in [[Hydrothermal vent|hydrothermal vents]] and [[Cold seep|cold seeps]]. These may be more akin to ancestral species.

There are about 1600 whale deaths per year, each lasting several decades. The distance between them might only be 10s of kms. 

Other creatures are either too small, and consumed quickly, or their bones are not nutrient-rich. Whales have cartilage-rich bones so they can dive into the deep ocean without breaking their ribs.
