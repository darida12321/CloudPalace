A powerful explosion of a [[Star|star]]. It's triggered by the re-ignition of nuclear fusion in a [[White dwarf|white dwarf]], or the [[Gravity|gravitational]] collapse of a massive [[Star|star]]'s core.

It has several types:
- [[Type 1a supernova]]: A [[White dwarf|white dwarf]] feeds on another star, [[Fusion|fusion]] reignites and explodes.
