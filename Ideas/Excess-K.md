The **excess-K** representation stores [[Signed integer representation|signed integers]] much the same way as [[Two's complement|two's complement]] does. Here, a number $n$ is represented by $n+K$. So $0$ is represented as $K$, and $-K$ is represented as an all-zero pattern.

The [[Two's complement|two's complement]] is basically an **excess-2^(n-1)** with the most significant bit flipped.

This representation is mostly used for representing [[Floating-point representation|floating-point numbers]]
