The **ones' complement** representation stores [[Signed integer representation|signed integers]] by flipping (**complementing**) positive numbers to get negative ones. 1 byte can store numbers between `-127` and `127`.
- ` 6`: `00000110` 
- `-6`: `11111001`

Problems:
- 0 is represented in two different ways (-0 and 0)
- In addition, you have to add the carry bit back into the number.
