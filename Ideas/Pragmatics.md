The study of how context shapes meaning. "Some of the students passed" implies that not all did, even though it didn't explicitly say that.

Grace's **cooperative principle** has 4 maxims: be informative, be truthful, be relevant, be clear. This is why sarcasm communicates something extra rather than being the default.

**Deixis** is when the meaning of phrases depends on who's speaking, when and where (here, you, today). We also have [[Speech act theory|speech act theory]] claiming that speaking is sometimes the same as doing an action.
