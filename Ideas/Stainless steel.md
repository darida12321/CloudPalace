It's [[Steel|steel]] that contains [[Chromium|chromium]] (over 10.5%), making it resistant to rust and corrosion. It can be alloyed with other materials to give it various properties.

- **Austenitic**: Obtained by alloying with [[Nickel|nickel]]. Most common variant.
	- **18/10 stainless steel**: 18% [[Chromium|chromium]], 10% [[Nickel|nickel]]
	- **18/8 stainless steel**: 18% [[Chromium|chromium]], 8% [[Nickel|nickel]]
	- **18/0 stainless steel**: 18% [[Chromium|chromium]], 0% [[Nickel|nickel]]
