This is a psychological effect changing your perception of an ambiguous object based on external stimuli or context. Some effects include:
- [[Brainstorm vs green needle]]: The same sound being heard in different ways.
- [[Laurel vs Yanny]]: Again, the same sound heard in two different ways.
- [[Checker shadow illusion]]: The same colour can be perceived as two different colours.
- [[McGurk effect]]: Some sounds can sound different paired with different lip movement.
- [[The dress]]: A picture of a dress that can either be blue/black or white/gold.
