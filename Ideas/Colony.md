Colonies are made up of two or more animals of the same species. There are many types:
- **Social colony**: Slime molds that cooperate, insects, and even birds and mammals.
- **Clonal colony**: A group of clones physically connected, or living in close proximity.
- **Colonial organism**: A group of closely connected clonal colonies.
- **Microbial colony**: A visible cluster of microorganisms growing from a single cell.

In contrast, there are **solitary organisms**, like jaguars, that do not associate with colonies, and all individuals have all the functions to survive and reproduce.
