Wheat has two proteins: **glutenin** and **gliadin**. When mixed with [[Water|water]], these link together into a protein matrix called **gluten**. Air bubbles can form in this matrix and expand, making that bready spongy structure.

The air bubbles can be created in multiple ways:
- For **leavened breads**, it's made by [[Yeast|yeast]].
- For **quick breads**, it's made with [[Baking soda|baking soda]] which is a base, and an **acid**.

A downtime of quick breads is that the reaction is immediate, so the gluten has less time to form spongy bubbles.
