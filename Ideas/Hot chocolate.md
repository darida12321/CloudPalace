##### Ingredients
- 2 100% cacao baking chocolate bars.
- 1cup Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 1cup sugar
- 2tbsp [[Cornstarch|cornstarch]]
- 1/2tsp [[Salt|salt]]
##### Steps
1. Freeze chocolate bars for about 10 mins
2. Break into pieces, put in food processor with cocoa powder, sugar, cornstarch, salt.
3. Process until powdered. Store in airtight container for 3 months.
4. Add 1/2tbsp of mix to 1cup boiling milk, and stir until combined.
