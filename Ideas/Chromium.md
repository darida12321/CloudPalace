It's an element of the [[Periodic table|periodic table]]. **Cr**, [[Atomic number|atomic number]] 24. It's a steely-grey lustrous hard and brittle [[Metal|metal]]. It doesn't [[Tarnish|tarnish]], so it's used for its **corrosion resistance**. It's one of the **hardest** element (after [[Carbon|carbon]] and [[Boron|boron]])

##### Sources
[[Chromite|Chromite]]: $\ce{FeCr2O4}$

##### Usage
- Mostly used for [[Stainless steel|stainless steel]].
- Sometimes used as pigments.
