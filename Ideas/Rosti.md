##### Ingredients
- 2 [[Potato|potatoes]]
- [[Salt]] and [[Pepper|pepper]]
- 4tbsp [[Olive oil|olive oil]]
Optional filling:
- 1 medium onion
- 100g mushroom
- 2 garlic
- 1tsp thyme leaves

##### Steps
- If using filling, cook the onion and mushroom first, add garlic and thyme, set aside.
- Cut **potatoes** into thin strips using a mandoline.
- Heat 2sp **olive oil** on medium. Add the potatoes and the spices. Cook for 7 mins.
	- If using filling, only add half the potatoes, then the filling, then the other half.
- Slide on plate, put another plate on top, flip it, slide it back. Cook for 7 mins.

# Notes
Might be good to microwave for 5 minutes beforehand.
