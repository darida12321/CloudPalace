They are usually [[Filter feeder|filter feeders]], found in basically every part of the world.

Once they stick to something they are extremely hard to get rid off, and they can increase the weight and drag of boats by 60%.

##### Life cycle
Despite being [[Sessile|sessile]], they do not [[Broadcast spawning|broadcast spawn]], although some [[Sperm casting|sperm cast]].

Most species are [[Hermaphrodite|hermaphrodites]], but some are [[Dioecy|dioecy]] or [[Androdioecy|androdioecy]]. Barnacles have the largest penis to body ratios, with it being 8 times their body size. They poke it out and flail it around, because they have no eyes.

The eggs are kept inside the barnacle until they hatch, then they get released. Then they are in a free-floating larval state until they find a solid surface to latch onto, then release a super-strong biological cement and anchor onto it.


##### Examples
**Rhizocephala**: One of the rare parasitic barnacles. Latches onto crustaceans and exercise total control over their host including **parasitic castration**. They basically an organism the has the phenotype of the host and the genotype of the parasite.
