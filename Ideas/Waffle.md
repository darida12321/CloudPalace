##### Ingredients
Dry mix:
- 1cup flour
- 1/2tsp baking powder
- 1/4tsp baking soda
- 1/2tsp [[Salt|salt]]
- 1/2tbsp sugar
Wet mix
- 2 [[Egg|eggs]]
- 1/2cup [[Buttermilk|buttermilk]]
- 4tbsp melted [[Butter|butter]]
- 1cup ice-cold [[Club soda|club soda]]
- 1tsp vanilla extract
##### Steps
1. Preheat waffle iron on low heat.
2. Whisk the egg whites until stiff peaks form.
3. Whisk the egg yolk and buttermilk, add butter, fold the egg whites, fold in club soda.
4. Cook in the waffle iron for about 8 mins.
