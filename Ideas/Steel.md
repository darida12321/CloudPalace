An [[Iron|iron]]-[[Carbon|carbon]] [[Alloy|alloy]] with a [[Carbon|carbon]] content between 0.08% and 2%. It's a good middle ground in terms of hardness and ductility between [[Wrought iron|wrought iron]] and [[Cast iron|cast iron]].
