The study of literal, context-free meaning. Some examples for what this entails is:
- **Synonyms and antonyms**: Words having identical or opposite meanings.
- **Arrangement**: The difference between "Dog bites man" and "Man bites dog".
- **Entailment**: The sentence "I saw a poodle" entails that "I saw a dog".
- **Presupposition**: The question "Do you still smoke?" presupposes that you used to smoke.
- **Meaning**: What "bird" means. There is a gradient of "bird-ness" instead of a strict rule.
