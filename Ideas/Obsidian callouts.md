**Callouts** are an extension of [[Markdown quote|quote blocks]]. They are created by adding `[!info]` to the beginning of the first line, then a `-` to make it foldable, then a custom title.

```
> [!info]- Custom callout title
> Hi, this is an information callout block.
```

> [!info]- Custom callout title
> Hi, this is an information callout block.
