These are [[Filter feeder|filter feeder]] organisms living in shallow water, but some live in the deep ocean where they form a mouth that can capture larger prey. They can be solitary or [[Colony|colonial]], where they can reach up to a metre in size.

##### Life cycle
They reproduce via [[Broadcast spawning|broadcast spawning]] or [[Egg brooding|egg brooding]]. They have a larva stage where they swim and search for a suitable location where they can settle and metamorphosize into their adult [[Sessile|sessile]] form.

Notably, the larvae have a nerve cord that shrinks and gets absorbed after settling.
