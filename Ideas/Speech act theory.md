When you say "I promise", you're not just conveying information, you're performing an action.

Every utterance has layers:
- **Locutionary Act**: The words themselves.
- **Illusionary Act**: The act you're performing (promising, warning, ordering, etc)
- **Perlocutionary Act**: The effect it has on the listener.

The sentence "Can you pass the salt" is literally a question about your abilities, but performs an act of requesting, and has the effect of getting the salt passed.
