##### Ingredients
Dough:
- 3 [[Egg|eggs]]
- 1/2cup brown sugar
- 3/4cup [[Buttermilk|buttermilk]]
- 2tsp [[Salt|salt]]
- 2tsp instant [[Yeast|yeast]]
- 6tbsp melted [[Butter|butter]]
- 4cup [[Flour|flour]]
Pecan-caramel sauce:
- 4tbsp [[Butter|butter]]
- 2/3cup brown sugar
- 3tbsp [[Buttermilk|buttermilk]]
- 1cup toasted pecans, coarsely chopped
- pinch of [[Salt|salt]]
Filling:
- 2/3cup brown sugar
- 1tbsp cinnamon
- 2tbsp melted [[Butter|butter]]
Orange-cream cheese glaze (optional):
- 120g cream cheese
- 1/4cup [[Buttermilk|buttermilk]]
- 1.5cup sugar
- 1tbsp orange zest (1 orange)
- 2tbsp orange juice
- pinch of [[Salt|salt]]

##### Steps
1. Make the dough:
	1. Whisk eggs in large bowl. Add sugar, buttermilk, salt, yeast, butter and whisk.
	2. Add flour and stir until a cohesive ball.
	3. Knead on floured surface for 2 mins until smooth and silky.
	4. Cover with wrap, let rise for 2h.
2. Make the pecan-caramel sauce:
	1. Cook the butter and sugar on medium-high until bubbling.
	2. Add buttermilk, pecans, salt. Stir to combine.
	3. Pour it to the bottom of a 33cm x 23cm baking dish.
3. Roll the dough and add filling:
	1. Move the dough to a floured surface, flour it.
	2. Roll it into a 40x30cm rectangle.
	3. Brush with molten butter, leaving a 4cm gap on a short edge
	4. Mix the cinnamon and sugar, sprinkle it throughout. 
	5. Roll the dough along its short end. Keep it seam-down.
	6. Cut into 12 even slices. Add them to the baking dish.
	7. Let it rise for 2h.
4. Cook the rolls:
	1. Preheat oven to 180C.
	2. Cook until golden brown, about 30 mins.
5. Optional glaze:
	1. Combine cream cheese, buttermilk, sugar, orange zest, orange juice and salt.
	2. Cook over medium, whisking until simmering.
	3. Spoon half on the bun, keep the rest table-side.
