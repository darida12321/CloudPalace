There are four **fundamental forces** in physics:
- [[Gravity]]: The weakest force, but it only attracts so it dominates over large scales.
- [[Electromagnetism]]: Opposite charges attract so they usually cancel out over larger scales.
- [[Weak force]]: Extremely short ranged, so only shows up in [[Beta radiation|beta radiation]].
- [[Strong force]]: Strongest force. Keeps [[Proton|protons]] together in [[Atom|atoms]].

These all have charges on which they act, and have carrying particles. **Gravity** is the odd one out. It's far weaker than the rest, and as such it's hard to unify it with [[Quantum mechanics|quantum mechanics]]. Instead, it's described in [[General relativity|general relativity]].
