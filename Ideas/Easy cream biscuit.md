##### Ingredients
- 2cup [[Flour|flour]]
- 1tbsp [[Baking powder|baking powder]]
- 3/4tsp [[Salt|salt]]
- 4tbsp melted [[Butter|butter]]
- 1.25cup heavy cream
##### Steps
1. Preheat oven to 220C
2. Whisk flour, baking powder and salt. Add 2tbsp butter and cream.
3. Knead on a floured surface. Roll until 2cm thick.
4. Cut out 7.5cm circles (about 8 in total) and put them on parchment paper.
5. Brush the top with the remaining molten butter. 
6. Bake for 15 mins, rotate pan halfway, cool for 5 mins.
##### Notes
Add 3tbsp sugar in step 2 and fold 1/2cup currants for a scone.
