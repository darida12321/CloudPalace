The study of how words and [[Morpheme|morphemes]] combine to form larger units like sentences.

Sentences are not flat structures, but rather hierarchical ones. When saying "The old man and women", it's not clear whether or not the women are old two. That's because there are two different possible underlying structures.

**Syntax** is not the same as meaning (that's [[Semantics|semantics]]). You can construct sentences like "Colourless green ideas sleep furiously" which make grammatical sense, but have no meaning.
