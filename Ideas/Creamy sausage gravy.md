##### Ingredients
- 1tbsp [[Butter|butter]]
- 10.5kg bulk sausage
- 1 small onion
- 2tbsp [[Flour|flour]]
- 2cup [[Milk|whole milk]]
- [[Salt|salt]] and [[Pepper|pepper]]
##### Steps
1. Heat the butter on medium high until foamy.
2. Add sausage, use wooden spatula to break up meat. cook for 6 mins.
3. Add onion, cook for 2 mins
4. Add flour, cook while stirring until absorbed (1 min)
5. Add the milk, whisking constantly. 
6. Simmer while whisking until thickened (3 mins)
7. Season with salt and lots of pepper.
