A **star** is a sphere of [[Plasma|plasma]] held together by [[Gravity|gravity]]. They are held up by the equilibrium of the attraction [[Gravity|gravity]], and the repulsion of [[Fusion|fusion]].

Their life begins with the gravitational collapse of a [[Nebula|nebula]]. Then it [[Fusion|fuses]] lighter elements like [[Hydrogen|hydrogen]] and [[Helium|helium]] into heavier ones, releasing energy. After [[Iron|iron]], fusion takes energy instead of giving it. Thus [[Fusion|fusion]] stops, the star collapses causing a [[Supernova|supernova]], and turns into a [[White dwarf|white dwarf]], [[Neutron star|neutron star]] or a [[Black hole|black hole]].
