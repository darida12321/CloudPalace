##### Ingredients
- 1/2cup [[Buttermilk|buttermilk]]
- 1/2cup sour cream
- 2cup [[Flour|flour]]
- 1tbsp [[Baking powder|baking powder]]
- 1/4tsp [[Baking soda|baking soda]]
- 8tbsp cold butter
- 2tbsp melted butter
##### Steps
1. Preheat oven to 220C
2. Whisk buttermilk and sour cream.
3. Combine flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and mix.
4. Add cubed cold butter, pulse a blender until the butter bits are small.
5. Add buttermilk mix to the flour mix, fold with spatula.
6. Knead on a floured surface until the mixture comes together.
7. Roll the dough into a 30cm square. Fold it to the centre 3x3 square (9 layers) twice.
8. Cut out 10cm circles and put them on baking sheet.
9. Brush the top of the biscuit with melted butter. 
10. Bake for 15 mins, rotate the pan halfway, let it cool for 5 more.
##### Notes
At the second folding, you can add some topping:
- 6oz grated cheddar and 1/4cup sliced scallions
- 1/2cup crumbled bacon and 2ox [[Parmigiano-Reggiano]]. Sprinkle top with more cheese.
- Add 2tbsp sugar in step 3, and add 1cup frozen or fresh berries. Sprinkle with more sugar.
