It is the strongest [[Fundamental forces|fundamental force]], responsible for interactions between [[Quark|quarks]], forming [[Proton|protons]] and [[Neutron|neutrons]]. 

It has three charges: red, green and blue. The dynamics are described in [[Quantum chromodynamics|quantum chromodynamics (QCD)]]. In practice the charges attract so much that outwardly they always show a neutral charge. The [[Nucleus|nucleus]] is held together by the **residual strong force**, or [[Nuclear force|nuclear force]].
