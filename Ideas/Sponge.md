One of the first animals to exist. They don't do much except [[Filter feeder|filter feed]] and reproduce. Some species stay small because of the [[Square cube law|square cube law]], but others have a fractal-like inside, so it can grow indefinitely. 

Each part of them can independently maintain itself, so a part of sponge breaking off is technically a type of asexual reproduction.

Their cells don't bind to each other, sot they constantly reorganize themselves. This is great if water currents change.
