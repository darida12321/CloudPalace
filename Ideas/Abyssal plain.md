Cold, still, dark, but one of the largest biomes on Earth.

The extreme pressure changes how proteins fold, so new species find it hard to adopt, and old species don't change much. This results in some of the most ancient species found here.

Creatures here had to adapt to the lack of food and solid ground.
- **Sea cucumbers**: Vacuum up organic waste and particles.
- **Tripod fish**: Stand on the ocean floor to conserve energy. Higher to catch faster currents.
- **Dumbo octopus**: Uses fins to move instead of limbs to conserve energy.
- **Sea spiders**: Expressing deep-sea gigantism. Uses vibrations to sense.
