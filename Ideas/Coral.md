Corals seems like one big thing. But it's actually a collection of microscopic **polyps**. They are [[Suspension feeder|suspension feeders]] using stinging tentacles. They also use mucus to trap smaller creature, and drag them into the polyps.

For some algae cells, instead of consuming them, they capture it via **symbiosome** cells. Through these they also gain the ability to [[Photosynthesis|photosynthesise]].

When temperatures get too high, the algae cells produce chemicals that are toxic to themselves and the coral, leading to rejection via a process called **bleaching**. It's called that because the transparent coral cells give way to the white calcium skeleton.

##### Life cycle
They are [[Sessile|sessile]] and [[Broadcast spawning|broadcast spawn]] to reproduce. The eggs and sperm float to the surface, where they find a match. Once they mature, they turn into **larvae** that are covered in hairs that wave around, causing it to move around. Then it sticks down and turns into a **polyp**.

Their bottom is capable of **biomineralization**, creating calcium carbonate crystals that act as a skeleton. The only living part of the coral is its skin, the rest is just the calcium skeleton. 

Some corals are fine like that, but **reef-building corals** create clones of themselves by budding off to the side, or elongating and splitting. They create water current highways to divert water to polyps in need, and can also share food with each other.
