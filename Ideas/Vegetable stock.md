##### Ingredients
- 3tbsp unflavoured [[Gelatin|gelatin]]
- 2 large onion, roughly chopped
- 2 large carrot, roughly chopped
- 4 stalk celery, roughly chopped
- 1 leek, trimmed
- 250g mushrooms
- 2 Granny Smith apple, quartered
- 2 bay leaves
- 2tsp whole black peppercorn
- 1tsp fennel seed
- 1tsp coriander seed
- 6 sprig parsley
##### Steps
1. 4 cup water and gelatin. Set aside for 10 mins.
2. Add everything to a dutch oven, add cold water to cover the ingredients.
3. Simmer for 1h. Add water if necessary.
4. Drain through fine-mesh strainer and cheese-cloth. 
5. Reduce to 2l, about 20 mins.
##### Notes
Will keep for 5 days, or 3 months frozen.
