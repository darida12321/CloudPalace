Also known as **flatware**. A few general factors:
- **Weighty** cutlery items are better. Thin and light ones feel cheaper. 
- **Balance** is important. for thin handles, it's achieved by a  flared back.
- Knives should have a wider handle cause you'll apply force with your finger.
- It should be made of [[Stainless steel|stainless steel]] (18/10 if possible).
- There are no size or design constraints. Personal preferences decide the best shape.

##### Types
There are only a few types typically needed, but dozens of variations exist.
- **Table spoon**: Stew and soup.
- **Table fork**: Main courses.
- **Table knife**: Cutting, packing things on fork.
- **Tea spoon**: Dessert or tea.
