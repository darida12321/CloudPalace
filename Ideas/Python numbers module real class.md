Inherits from **[[Python numbers module complex class|numbers module complex class]]**. Standardises the operations on [[Python float|floats]].

**Fields**:
- `numerator`: The numerator, an [[Python numbers module integral class|integral]].
- `denominator`: The denominator, an [[Python numbers module integral class|integral]].
**Functions**:
- `conjugate()`: The complex conjugate
**Operations**:
- `float()`: Conversion to [[Python float|float]]
- `//`, `%`, `<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`.
- `round()`: Rounding a number.
- `divmod()`: Get quotient and remainder.
- `math.trunc()`: Truncate, from the [[Python math module|math module]].
- `math.floor()`: Round down, from the [[Python math module|math module]].
- `math.ceil()`: Round up, from the [[Python math module|math module]].
