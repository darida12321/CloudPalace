The **competitive exclusion principle** (or **Gause's law**) states that species competing for the same limited resource cannot coexist. One specie with a small advantage will dominate the others long term.

While it is a nice theory, there are many counterexamples in nature due to:
- Resource partitioning.
- Competition-colonization tradeoff.
- Trophic interactions ([[Autotroph|autotrophs]], [[Heterotroph|heterotroph]]).
