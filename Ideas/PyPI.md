The **Python Package Index (PyPI)** is a [repository](https://github.com/pypi) for distributing [[Python package|Python packages]]. Its backend is called [warehouse](https://github.com/pypi/warehouse), which was a recent overhaul.

A useful counterpart to this is **TestPyPI**. It acts just like Pypi, but it lets you edit and delete your uploads, which is great for testing.
