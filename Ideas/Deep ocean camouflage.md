While there's significantly less sunlight in the [[Deep ocean|deep ocean]], creatures in the [[Twilight zone|twilight zone]] can still see the silhouette of fishes.

##### Normal camouflage
Some creatures adapt by having lighter pigments on their underside. This makes them blend in from all angles.

Some squids mimic other creatures in movement and pigmentation, or leave behind ink that resembles the shape of other animals.

##### Counter-illumination
Some creatures use bioluminescence to produce their own light, blending into the environment. They can change how much light they produce based on their depth.

##### Silvering
Creating a reflective shell that reflects the water back to them. Often achieved by microscopic guanine crystals, or proteins called reflectins.

##### Transparency
There are no harmful UV lights in the deep ocean damaging DNA, so creatures can turn completely translucent.

However, the concentration of bioluminescent food they eat will show up. Some combat this with a red tint, because red light doesn't make it deep into the water.
