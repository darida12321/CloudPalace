Here, there is no more light whatsoever. As such, the [[Diel vertical migration|Diel vertical migration]] cannot take effect. It is also significantly colder, around 4C, due to the [[Thermocline|thermocline]] of the ocean. This also causes [[Deep-sea gigantism]] The increased pressure requires the development of a different set of proteins. Animals push their morphology to the extreme to gather food and reproduce.

Due to the lack of food, predators are forced to develop sit and wait tactics. Instead of camouflage, bioluminescence is used to lure prey. As for actual camouflage, they turn dark brown or black colours. Sometimes reflecting less than 0.5% of light.

There is no need for fast swimming, so fishes don't have a streamlined body shape. They are either circular like the **anglerfish**, or elongate like the **sawtooth eel** or **snipe eel**, which is the most energy-efficient locomotion.

Other environments offer some topology, but here there is none. Some organisms end up sticking to larger organisms. These can be parasites, harmless shelter-seekers or symbiotes.

##### Life
- **Greenland sharks**: Live for centuries due to slow metabolism, and grow up to 7m.
- **Comb jelly**: Extended tentacles retract quickly at the presence of any food.
- **Angler fish**: Extreme sexual dimorphism. The male is smaller, has large eyes and nostrils to pick up pheromones, attaches itself with denticles, their circulatory systems combine, and the male devolves into a sperm-producing sac.
- **Snipe eel**: Extremely long fish. It has the most vertebrae of any animal.
- **Dragonfish**: Has red bioluminescent search lights under eyes.
- **Dumbo octopus**: Uses fins to move instead of tentacle-jets. 
- **Strawberry squid**: Big eye up to spot silhouettes. Small eye down for bioluminescence.
- **Black-eyed squid**: Carries a sac of eggs around for 9 months. She cannot eat since tentacles are occupied, but must swim to oxygenate the eggs.
- **Deepstaria jelly**: Captures prey like a bag and digests it.
- **Anuropus amphipod**: Parasitic and eats **Deepstaria jelly**, but doesn't cut a hole.
- **Giant phantom jelly**: One of the largest jellyfish, to increase surface area. Doesn't sting.
- **Pelagic Brotula**: Partners with **Giant phantom jelly** to ward off parasites and get shelter.
- **Praya dubia**: a [[Siphonophore|siphonophore]] that can reach 50m of length.
