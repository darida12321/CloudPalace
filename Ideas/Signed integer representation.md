While [[Unsigned integer representation|unsigned integers]] are easy to represent, there are a few more options for **signed integers**. 

There are many ways of representing this. The most common one is [[Two's complement|two's complement]], but historically the [[Sign-magnitude representation|sign-magnitude representation]] and [[Ones' complement|one's complement]] was also used, although doing arithmetic on them is harder, and they represent 0-s in two distinct way.

A generalization of the ideas behind [[Two's complement|two's compliment]] is [[Excess-K|excess-K]], but it's not backwards compatible with [[Unsigned integer representation|unsigned integers]]. It is, however, used for representing [[Floating-point representation|floating-point numbers]].
