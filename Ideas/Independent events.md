The [[Random event|events]], $A_1,A_2\dots A_n$ are **independent** iff:
$$P(\bigcap_{i\in S}A_i)=\prod_{i\in S} P(A_i)\qquad\text{for every subset }S\text{ of }\{1,2,\dots n\}$$

We can also describe this via [[Conditional probability|conditional probability]]: $P(A|B)=P(A)$.
3 piecewise **independent** event does not imply that they are independent.
