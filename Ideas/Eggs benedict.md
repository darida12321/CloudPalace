##### Ingredients
- 4 [[Poached egg|poached eggs]]
- [[Hollandaise sauce]]
- 2 English muffins split, roasted
- 2tsp vegetable oil
- 4 slices thick-cut ham
- Cayenne pepper
- Parsley leaves

##### Steps
1. Boil salted water, cook the poached eggs.
2. Cook the ham on vegetable oil. Then the muffins too in the same pan.
3. Make the Hollandaise sauce.
4. Muffin, bacon, egg, hollandaise, sprinkle of cayenne, parsley leaves.
