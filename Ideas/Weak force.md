It is the weakest [[Fundamental forces|fundamental force]], responsible for the radioactive beta-decay of [[Atom|atoms]].

It can be described on its own by [[Quantum flavordynamycs|quantum flavordynamics (QFD)]], but in practice it's often combined with [[Electromagnetism|electromagnetism]] and described in the [[Electroweak theory|electroweak theory (EWT)]].

It's effective range is less than the diameter of a proton, so it doesn't contribute much to observed phenomenon.
