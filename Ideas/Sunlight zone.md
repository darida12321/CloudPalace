It's teeming with life, due to the abundance of [[Photosynthesis|photosynthetic]] primary producers, [[Phytoplankton|phytoplankton]].

There's also **neustons**, animals living on the ocean-air interface. The **Portuguese man o' war** is a [[Siphonophore|siphonophore]] floating on the surface due to its gas-filled-bladder, feeding with 30m stinging tentacles.
