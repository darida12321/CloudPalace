They are multi-cellular [[Suspension feeder|suspension feeders]] that often look similar to [[Jellyfish|jellyfish]], but are extremely different in fundamental ways. They are kind of on the line between a [[Colony|colony organism]] and an individual.

##### Colony or individual
The cleanest example of a [[Colony|colony]] is a bacterial one. Here we recognize "the whole" as a single object, but also see that it's made of individuals that could survive and reproduce on their own. On the opposite end, a **human** is an **individual**. It is composed of parts, but they could not exist without one-another. Human-bits are found exclusively in a human.

These extremes form a spectrum.
- A **bacteria colony** is a colony where the parts are entirely distinct.
- A **zebra herd** is a colony, but its parts interact socially and rely on each other.
- A **bee colony** is a colony, but its parts couldn't live without each other.
- A [[Coral]] is a colony, where its parts are fused together and exchange nutrients.
- A **siphonophore** is (not?) a colony. Its parts are fused specialized individuals.
- A **human** is not a colony, its parts couldn't exist without the whole.

Its parts are technically animals, but couldn't live by themselves so don't classify as an organism. They are called [[Zooid|zooids]].

##### Structure
Siphonophores are composed of [[Zooid|zooids]]:
- **Gonophores**: Reproductive zooids releasing eggs/sperm, can be [[Hermaphrodite|hermaphrodite]] or [[Dioecy|dioecy]].
- **Nectophores**: Responsible for moving around.
- **Gastrozooids**: Stinging appendages.
- **Palopns**: Circulates digestive fluid along the stem.
- **Bracts**: Thick gelatinous overhang protecting other zooids, and keeping them buoyant.

There are also non-zooid parts:
- **Stem**: Where all the zooids attach.
- **Pneumatophore**: A gas-filled bladder orientating the siphonophore.

##### Examples
- **Portuguese man o' war**: Floats on the surface of the ocean, tendrils hanging down.  
- **Praya dubia**: It can reach 50m of length, making it the longest animal.
- **Flying spaghetti monster**: Lives in the [[Midnight zone|midnight zone]] of the ocean.
