The biological conversion of carbon-containing molecules (carbon-dioxide, methane) and nutrients into organic matter via the oxidation of inorganic compounds (hydrogen, hydrogen sulfide, ferrous ions).

Often used by deep-sea (and possibly sub-surface Martian) microorganisms. 

$$18H_2S+6CO_2+3O_2\rightarrow C_6H_{12}O_6\text{ (carbohydrate)}+12H_2O+18S$$
