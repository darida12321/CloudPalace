##### Ingredients
- 1 [[Egg|egg]]
- [[Salt|Salted]] [[Water|water]]
##### Steps
1. Boil salted water, then turn the heat off.
2. Crack eggs into separate bowls.
3. Use a fine-mesh strainer to separate the loose whites.
4. Gently lower the strainer into the pot, tilt the egg out.
5. Wait 1-2 minutes, swirling them occasionally.
6. Pick them out with a slotted spoon, transfer to paper-towel lined plate.



# Notes
We are basically making a [[Soft-boiled egg|soft-boiled egg]], but without its skin on. The main difficulty is keeping the egg egg-shaped without its shell.
- Fresh eggs have a firmer membrane around their whites.
- Boiling water agitates the egg, but even a sub-simmer is enough to cook them.
- Even fresh eggs have some loose whites. Crack it into a strainer to get rid of it.

You can keep poached eggs in the fridge, suspended in water, for a few days. Then reheat them in warm water and they are as good as new.
