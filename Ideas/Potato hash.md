##### Ingredients
- 3 [[Potato|potatoes]]
- [[Salt]] and [[Pepper|pepper]]
- 3tbsp [[Olive oil|olive oil]]
- 2 [[Bell pepper|bell peppers]]
- 1 onion
- Some hot sauce
- 3 eggs (optional)

##### Steps
- If using eggs, preheat oven to 200C
- Cut potatoes into cubes, boil or microwave them.
- Add 2tbsp oil on high heat, wait until it smokes, add the potatoes and stir until brown.
- Add peppers, onions, salt, pepper. Cook until onions brown. Add hot sauce.
- If using eggs, make holes, crack the egg in them, put into the oven until whites toughen.

# Notes
Potatoes can also be microwaved for 5 minutes beforehand.
