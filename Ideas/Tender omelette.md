##### Ingredients
- 3 [[Egg|eggs]], 1 yolk.
- 1/4 tsp [[salt]]. 1/4 tsp [[Pepper|pepper]]
- 1tbsp whole milk
- 1tbsp herbs (parsley, chives, etc)
- 1.5tbsp [[Butter|butter]]

##### Steps
1. Combine egg, yolk, salt, pepper, milk, herbs. Let it sit for 15 mins.
2. Add 1tbsp butter in small bits to egg. Melt remaining in non-stick pan on low heat.
3. Stir and cook until it can just barely hold its shape. Shake the pan to distribute egg evenly.
4. Tilt the pan so it's thicker on one side. remove from heat, cover 1min.
5. Roll it up starting on the thick side, and serve.

# Notes
Other fillings also easy. 
- **Mushroom, pepper, onion**: 1/2 cup each. Cook mushrooms, then add onion and pepper.
- **Asparagus, shallot, goat cheese**: 8 stalk asparagus, cut to 5cm bits. 5min. add shallot.
