##### Ingredients
- 1 [[Egg|egg]] yolk
- 1/3 tbsp lemon juice
- 1/3 tbsp hot water
- 75g [[Butter|butter]]
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
- Pinch of [[Salt|salt]]
##### Steps
1. Add the yolk, lemon juice and water to a cup.
2. Melt the butter until it's 80/90 degrees (just begins to bubble).
3. Pour the butterfat into a cup (leave the water behind), remove the floating milk proteins.
4. Start blending the yolk mixture, slowly add the butter. 

# Notes
Hollandaise sauce is an egg-stabilized emulsion of fat in water, like [[Mayonnaise|mayonnaise]]. However, hollandaise is made with butter-fat which solidifies below 35C. As such, it cannot be stored in the fridge.

Typically recipes tell you to heat the butter and egg, and combine them. It's enough to heat one, which should be the butter in order to separate out the butterfat.
