##### Ingredients:
- 1 [[Egg|egg]]
- 0.5l [[Water|water]]
##### Steps
1. Boil water.
2. Gently place eggs in the water.
3. After 30 seconds, add ice cubes or cold water. 
4. Bring water back to a sub-simmer, cook for 9 minutes.
5. Peel the eggs while they're warm, under running water.

# Notes
You want both the white and yolk to be firm, but not rubbery (75C).
- Keeping water exactly at 75C is a pain.
- Boiling it fast gives a temperature gradient which we don't want.
- Boiling it slow makes the yolk stick to the wall.
