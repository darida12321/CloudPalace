Diel means day in Latin, and this is the largest synchronous migration in the world.

Creatures in the [[Twilight zone|twilight zone]] have sensitive photoreceptors, and can determine The time of day. At night when predators are dormant, they surface to feast on [[Phytoplankton|phytoplankton]].

During WWII after the invention of **sonar**, people started picking up a false sea floor at a depth of 300-500m. This moved up and down throughout the day. This was caused by the insane amount of migrating biomass. It was named the **ECR layer**, or the **phantom bottom**.
