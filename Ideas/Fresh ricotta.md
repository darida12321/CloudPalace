##### Ingredients
Dry mix:
- 4cup [[Milk|whole milk]]
- 1/2tsp [[Salt|salt]]
- 1/4cup [[Vinegar|distilled white vinegar]]
##### Steps
- Line colander with 4 layers of cheese cloth.
- Combine milk, salt and vinegar, microwave on high for 4-6mins (75C)
- Stir for 5s. The milk should separate into solid white curds and [[Whey|whey]].
	- If not, microwave for 30s until fully separated.
- Add curds to the colander. Cover with plastic wrap, wait for it to drain.
- Store for up to 5 days.
##### Notes
The vinegar can be replaced with lemon juice from 2 lemons to make it a bit more sour.
