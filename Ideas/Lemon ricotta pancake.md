##### Ingredients
Dry mix:
- 1cup flour
- 1/2tsp baking powder
- 1/4tsp baking soda
- 1/2tsp [[Salt|salt]]
- 1/2tbsp sugar
Wet mix:
- 1.5cup buttermilk
- 1 cup [[Ricotta|ricotta]] (drained for 30min)
- 2tbsp [[Butter|butter]]
- 2 [[Egg|egg]]
- 1/2tsp vanilla extract

##### Steps
- Combine dry ingredients.
- Whisk buttermilk, ricotta, butter, eggs, vanilla extract. Add lemon zest.
- Add to the dry mix, gently. Don't overmix.
- Preheat nonstick skillet over medium for 5 mins. Cover with thin layer of oil.
- Make 8 pancakes. 2 mins on each side (bubble on top, brown on bottom)
