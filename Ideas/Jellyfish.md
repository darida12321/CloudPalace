There isn't a strict definition for what a **jellyfish** is, because many branches of evolution created similar traits. It's thought to be one of the oldest multi-cellular species, but they don't leave behind fossil records. 

They contain **cnidocytes** which are the stinging cells on their tentacles. If you're stung, do not pee on it. That's a myth.

##### Life cycle
They have one of the most bizarre life cycles, using both **asexual** and **sexual** reproduction.

Grown **jellyfish** can reproduce via [[Broadcast spawning|broadcast spawning]]. A fertilized egg becomes a **planula** that floats around until it fins a place to attach to. There they grow into an asexual polyp called a **scyphistoma**. That grows into a **strobila** which strobilates. Segments of the **strobila** pop off and become free-floating **ephyra**. These, at last, grow into full fledged **jellyfish**.

##### Examples
- **Lion's mane jellyfish**: The largest species, just over 2m wide.
- **Immortal jellyfish**: Adults can go back to their polyp stage if they feel stressed. This can occur because they are threatened, starving, or simply due to [[Senescence|senescence]].
- **Giant phantom jellyfish**: About 0.7m wide, but just beautiful.
